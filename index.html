The Venue

Older than the Christmas tree, the card and the cracker.

In 1707, a footman to Queen Anne named William Fortnum began selling on the palace's half-used candle ends. He went into business with his landlord, Hugh Mason. What started as a modest grocer on St James's Market became one of the most recognisable shops in the world.

Almost everything we now think of as Christmas (the decorated tree, the greetings card, the cracker) arrived in the 1840s. Fortnum's was well over a century old by then. Its hampers went to Waterloo, to Florence Nightingale in the Crimea, and up Everest in 1922; in the First World War, families sent Christmas hampers from Piccadilly to the trenches. The windows on that corner are, for a great many people, the moment December properly begins.

On the 1st of December, we have the bar on the third floor to ourselves: the 3'6 Bar, named for the three shillings and sixpence a guest cost at a Fortnum's cocktail party in the 1930s. Low light, soft music and a bar that knows exactly what it's doing.

Based in London and Essex, Charlene works with individuals and couples across the UK and internationally, helping them strengthen emotional connection, improve communication, and rebuild intimacy in their relationships.

Through her work as a therapist, speaker, and media commentator, she specialises in supporting people navigating intimacy challenges, loss of sexual desire, relationship conflict, dating patterns, and healing after break-ups. Details

Your questions, answered.

What's the dress code?

Festive, elevated, London in December. Velvet, tailoring, a good coat, something that catches the light. Not black-tie, and not the office. Dress like you're going somewhere worth photographing — because you are.

Where exactly is it?

The 3'6 Bar on the third floor of Fortnum & Mason, 181 Piccadilly, London W1A 1ER. Green Park and Piccadilly Circus are both a short walk. We strongly recommend public transport — parking in St James's in December is not your friend.

Can I bring a guest?

Invitation-only. Get in touch before booking to ask about bringing a guest — we'll confirm whether we can accommodate them.

What's included in the ticket price?

Exclusive access to the 3'6 Bar, a glass of sparkling wine on arrival, a drinks token for a second drink of your choosing, canapés through the afternoon, a pianist playing live, a fireside chat with Charlene Douglas, and something to take home.

Will there be music?

Yes — a pianist, live, through the afternoon. Pitched so the room sounds like December in Piccadilly, and still low enough that you never have to raise your voice to be heard.

Is this actually a networking event?

Not in the traditional sense. No pitches, no speed rounds, no badges with your elevator pitch printed on them. It's a connection-led afternoon, built around the conversations that happen when the right people are in the same room.

How many places are there?

Seventy. We won't stretch it to squeeze anyone in — the point is the quality of the room, not the size of it.

I have dietary requirements or allergies.

Please add them at checkout. Fortnum's need advance notice to cater properly, so don't skip this step if it applies to you.

What's the refund policy?

Due to venue and catering commitments, tickets are non-refundable. You may transfer your place to another eligible guest with prior approval — just get in touch before the day.
